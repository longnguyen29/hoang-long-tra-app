## Static quality review and corrections

A separate visual check identified weak A text/photo contrast, an unclear selected
marker, a mislabeled enlarged mobile-state study, mixed-language EN labels and
some design-jargon headings. The revision adds opaque text backing in A, geometric
selection dots, precise state-study labeling, translated EN labels and clearer
product/manufacturing copy. B's original split-like opening was revised further
into the photographic spread shown in the final exports.

Artifact checks validate exported dimensions, image embedding/source integrity,
DRAFT labels, local links, file scope, guard integrity, static palette contrast
and hashes. They do not establish a website accessibility or performance pass.
See [verification](concepts/round-2/artifact-verification.json). No application
build or business regression rerun is necessary for this concept-only update.
