## R1 — Core proposition

**A living Vietnamese tea house with the credibility of a manufacturer and the
utility of a procurement platform.**

For serious distributors, manufacturers, chains and importers, the House must
connect the physical leaf to a useful buying conversation: which tea, for what
application to evaluate, which packing/trade basis, and who handles the next step.
The premium heritage retail collection supports this identity; it is not an equal
commercial priority on the homepage.

“Procurement platform” describes decision utility, not a new ERP, portal or form.
Use the existing catalog, actual product identities, product-aware sample links,
company documents and existing enquiry. No new transaction system is proposed.
“World-class” is an internal design quality ambition, not a ranking or certificate.

## R2 — Three VI/EN opening concepts

### A. Continuity into a new market

VI: **Tiếp nghề trà Hà Giang. / Cùng bạn mở thị trường.**

Supporting sentence: Từ nền tảng gia đình làm trà từ năm 1995, Hoàng Long tiếp nối
nghề chế biến tại Tân Lập, Hà Giang. Cùng lựa chọn bốn dòng trà chủ lực, mẫu,
quy cách và lịch cung ứng cho nhu cầu kinh doanh.

EN, independently written: **Tea-making in Hà Giang. / A starting point for your next market.**

Support: Built on family tea-making experience since 1995, Hoàng Long processes
tea in Tân Lập, Hà Giang. Explore four core selections, then discuss samples,
packing and a supply plan for your business.

CTA: **Trao đổi mẫu & báo giá / Request samples & a quotation**.
Strength: specific place and continuity. Tradeoff: a slower commercial opening.
[Desktop](concepts/round-2/a-opening-1440.png) · [Mobile](concepts/round-2/a-opening-390.png).

### B. Producer and market partner — recommended

VI: **Nhà làm trà. / Bạn phát triển thị trường.**

Full support: Kinh nghiệm gia đình làm trà từ năm 1995. Chế biến tại Tân Lập,
Hà Giang. Bốn dòng trà chủ lực để cùng lựa chọn mẫu, quy cách và điều kiện cung ứng
cho kế hoạch kinh doanh của bạn.

Mobile support, as drawn: Gia đình làm trà từ 1995. Bốn dòng trà để chọn mẫu,
quy cách và bàn kế hoạch cung ứng. Tân Lập, Hà Giang is separately labelled beside
the photograph; the sentence need not repeat every fact.

EN: **We make tea. / You build the market.**

Support: Family tea-making experience since 1995. Processing in Tân Lập, Hà Giang.
Four core teas, with samples, bulk packing and commercial terms discussed around
your import, distribution or beverage brief.

CTA: **Trao đổi mẫu & báo giá / Request samples & a quotation**.
Secondary: **Khám phá bốn dòng trà / Explore the four teas**.

“Nhà” makes the House identity useful: a producer role in a commercial relationship.
It is a role proposition, not a promise of OEM capability, market success, factory
ownership or manufacturing every item in the wider retail catalog. The four-tea
index and buyer field sheet must immediately make the proposition concrete.

VI uses “Nhà làm trà. / Bạn phát triển thị trường.” across two desktop lines; mobile
splits the producer phrase beside the real tree photograph and places the market
phrase below. English has its own type size, two-line desktop setting and shorter mobile measure.
The English hero studies translate navigation, kicker, supporting copy and captions;
canonical Vietnamese tea names retain identity. These remain static concepts, not
a complete translated website or runtime locale QA result.

### C. Product-first invitation

VI: **Mật. Khói. Mộc. Ngọc Lan. / Bắt đầu từ một mẫu trà.**

Support: Bốn dòng trà chủ lực của Hoàng Long, phát triển từ nền tảng gia đình làm
trà từ năm 1995 và hoạt động chế biến tại Tân Lập, Hà Giang. Cùng chọn sản phẩm và
xác lập nhu cầu cung ứng trước khi chốt báo giá.

EN: **Four teas. Distinct characters. / Start with a sample.**

Support: Honey-Note Black Tea, Smoky Black Tea, Mộc Green Tea and Magnolia-Scented
Green Tea. Explore Hoàng Long’s core range, then discuss the sample and supply
requirements for your business.

CTA: **Trao đổi mẫu & báo giá / Request samples & a quotation**.
Strength: most explicit product proposition. Tradeoff: full names take space,
and aromatic shorthand must never imply added honey or smoking ingredients.
English uses full names in support instead of forcing Vietnamese shorthand into
an ambiguous English headline. Visual concept C is shown in the comparison.

## R6 — Make the buyer's first visit useful

The hero contains a compact supply/contact field: **PP/PE 20/30/35 kg · EXW
Tân Lập · Anh Long 078 229 3579**. The initial view also exposes the four tea names.
The selected-product chapter then joins a large real photograph, confirmed notes,
trial application, packing and product-aware sample action. The visitor can skip
storytelling and reach the existing commercial flow without registration.

| Buyer question | Visible answer / existing route |
| --- | --- |
| What teas? | Four canonical profiles: Mật, Khói, Mộc, Ngọc Lan; all names visible in the material index, no carousel discovery. |
| Suitable for what? | Application as a sample-evaluation purpose. Mật has owner-confirmed pronounced aroma suited to beverages. Other notes support a shortlist, not a yield/compatibility guarantee. |
| How supplied? | Bulk PP/PE 20/30/35 kg, adjustable; EXW factory at Tân Lập. Confirm actual specification, quantity, timing and responsibilities in the quotation. |
| What next? | Selected tea → existing product-aware sample/quote helper → `/wholesale#trade-brief`; company profile and dated PDFs provide deeper detail. |
| Who handles it? | Named existing export-development contact plus the House office channel. No invented staff biography, new signing authority or partner rights. |

No new homepage form, stock assertion, MOQ guarantee, certification badge,
reference-client strip or live availability API. The [acceptance scenarios](../qa/visual-acceptance.md)
cover importer, manufacturer/chain, distributor and mobile visitor explicitly.

## R5 — An honest first edition, not a fake complete factory tour

Release A can be a complete introductory homepage with the existing tree portrait,
leaf-handling photograph, four catalog photos and equipment collage. It cannot be
marketed as a complete factory assessment, QC documentary, shipment traceability
viewer or named-maker portrait series. Release B requires real commissioned
photographs and their provenance; photographs alone do not prove certifications
or audited capacity. See the prioritized [photographic readiness brief](photographic-readiness.md).
