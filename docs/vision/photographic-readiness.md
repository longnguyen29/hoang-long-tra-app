## Release A — Complete introduction, bounded evidence

Available: tree portrait `/landing/1.jpg`, leaf-handling `/landing/2.jpg`, four
unmodified catalog photographs `/export/core-*.jpeg`, archive equipment collage
`/landing/3.jpg`. Use original photographs and truthful crops. A page using these
can honestly introduce the House, its four core products, owner-supplied supply
basis and an enquiry route. The comps show this edition, not invented future photos.

| Chapter | Ready for A | Must not be described as complete |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Origin/craft | Real tree/handling views, family experience since 1995, literal captions | A documented estate survey, exact tree age/elevation, full named operation or factory provenance audit. |
| Four teas | Dry-leaf photographs and owner-confirmed sensory distinctions | Comparable brewed-liquor imagery, measured yield/colour/stability or lab specification. |
| Manufacturing | Existing two-photo equipment archive shown intact, with dated-context limitation | A current operating-factory panorama, complete production tour, equipment-origin/ownership proof or audited capacity. |
| Quality/packing | Accurate text about topics to agree, PP/PE 20/30/35kg and EXW quotation basis | A documentary QC/packing demonstration, certification or ready-stock confirmation. |
| People | Public commercial contact as a typography-led block | A portrait series or personal biography without a real approved image/role source. The hand photo is not a named portrait. |

No AI manufacturing evidence, synthetic estates/workers, false portraits,
third-party cafe photos as client proof, customer logos without permission, or
blank stock-image placeholders on a finished release.

Shoot practical alternate crops for 390px and 1440px instead of assuming one wide
image works everywhere. Avoid over-styled ceremonies, generic regional costumes
or unverifiable cultural motifs. Do not stage an unavailable industrial operation
as ordinary practice. Preserve originals privately; publish optimized derivatives
with an accurate caption and rights record, removing unnecessary location metadata.

## Readiness gate

B is not “complete” until each claimed chapter has its corresponding real asset,
context and permission checked. Unavailable chapters remain bounded A text/image
compositions; they do not acquire a green completion badge. Test reports,
certificates, export-market/customer claims and capacity audits require their own
issuer/entity/date/product/lot scope. Photography cannot substitute for them.
