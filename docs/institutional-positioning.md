## Evidence to prepare privately

The user asked whether old invoices count. They are useful transaction records; a coherent historical shipment dossier is more informative than a standalone invoice. Collect only actual documents, confirm the exact legal entity and role in the transaction, and redact third-party confidential information before any disclosure.

| Evidence group | Useful actual material | Presentation boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entity and remit | Registration record, tax details, contact remit or appointment | Do not infer statutory signing authority from a website title. |
| Production | Dated factory/equipment photographs, processing flow, production records, basis for capacity planning | Do not imply all output is immediately available or all machinery is owned by the trading entity. |
| Product and quality | Technical specification, reference sample/lot, actual laboratory report with date/scope; any valid certificate with issuer, scope and expiry | Do not turn a past report into certification for every product or current shipment. |
| Transaction experience | Actual commercial invoice plus matching packing list, transport document, customs record/delivery acknowledgement where available; references used with permission | An internal preliminary printout is not independent evidence of a completed export. Historical documents do not prove current capacity or compliance. |

A public summary can show checked company facts and selected permitted evidence. Detailed invoices, customer identities, contracts, prices and bank/payment records should remain private. This release adds no public document-upload portal and publishes no customer dossier. The existing company-documents tool remains a template/export workflow.

## Research basis

- [THIE — Quality assurance](https://thie-online.eu/quality-assurance/): supports sample evaluation and quality checks as useful tea-trade presentation topics.
- [CBI — Buyer requirements in Europe](https://www.cbi.eu/buyer-requirements-europe): distinguishes market requirements from additional buyer requirements. Requirements must be established for the actual product and destination.
- [International Trade Administration — Common export documents](https://www.trade.gov/common-export-documents): distinguishes invoice, packing list and transport/origin documents. Used to explain the evidence categories, not as a statement of which US rules apply to Vietnamese exports.

These sources inform document structure. They are not proof of Hoàng Long's own certifications or trading history.
