# Company profile and trade catalogue

Public `/company` introduces Hoàng Long in Vietnamese and English, with a dated profile/catalogue PDF in each language. `/catalog` continues reading eligible, available tea products from the existing `catalog_products` table. Homepage navigation, trade desk and event catalogue link to the profile.

Public contact details reuse `lib/event-contact.js`. Company copy in `lib/company-profile.js` carries already published history, raw-material regions and declared production capacity, with volume/delivery confirmed per order. The Hanoi address is explicitly an office/warehouse. The owner supplied Công ty TNHH Thương Mại Trà Hoàng Long, tax ID0110818609, factory/EXW location Tân Lập, Hà Giang and the export-development contact. These are owner-supplied facts, not independent registry/certification verification. No certification, tested standard or export-market achievement was invented. Requested certificates/test reports are items to confirm, not claims of availability.

`lib/trade-product-details.js` classifies known existing teas from their catalogue names, translates four names missing English, and provides editorial sample-evaluation guidance separately from actual product notes/flavours. Actual brewing and listed packing fields are reused. Listed retail/product packing is not bulk MOQ. Grade, bulk MOQ, lead time and shelf life remain quotation-specific until entered and confirmed. Pu-erh pieces are not converted to kilograms without a unit weight.

No migration is required. Product data remains editable through the existing product tools. The web catalogue refreshes from the public product table. PDFs are dated snapshots, not live stock or binding quotations; they direct buyers to the web catalogue and an enquiry. The document generator accepts only a public bilingual JSON payload; do not supply customer data, internal price sheets or credentials.

Existing sample/quote submissions, consent, Pipeline and notifications are retained. Profile/catalog links preserve a valid language and approved campaign parameters; visitor information is not placed in navigation URLs. No customer communications or social posts are sent by this feature.

The institutional edition is described in `docs/institutional-positioning.md`. Its main public entry is `/company`, with cooperation brief at `#cooperation` and the named export contact at `#contact`. Homepage, B2B desk, catalogue, static export desk, metadata and downloadable PDFs share this direction. Public title/contact changes do not create a partner account or grant access, referral ownership or commission rights.

The deeper edition adds `/company#portfolio`, `#operating-model` and `#quality`, with a six-link section index, three buyer-oriented product segments, concrete responsibilities along the supply chain and four expandable technical assessment topics. `/story` gives the origin/processing context without implying all commercial-catalogue products share the same origin. The bilingual PDFs reproduce the expanded institutional content and retain all current public products.
